# Harmonic Spectral Measurements

This section lists the exact fundamental and harmonic voltage spectral amplitudes extracted from the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) of the sampled output in Cadence Virtuoso:

- **Fundamental Component ($A_1$)**:
  - Measured Value: **$498.2788\text{ mV}$**
  - Voltage Representation: $0.4982788\text{ V}$
- **Third Harmonic ($A_3$)**:
  - Measured Value: **$78.09553\ \mu\text{V}$**
  - Voltage Representation: $78.09553 \times 10^{-6}\text{ V}$
- **Fifth Harmonic ($A_5$)**:
  - Measured Value: **$13.63941\ \mu\text{V}$**
  - Voltage Representation: $13.63941 \times 10^{-6}\text{ V}$

These parameters confirm that the third and fifth harmonics are highly suppressed, indicating excellent tracking linearity.
